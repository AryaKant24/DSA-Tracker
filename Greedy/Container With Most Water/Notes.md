# Notes

## Approach
Two Pointer
1. Take minimum of two heights as initial volume and multiply by length
2. Try to maximise height at each step and calculate volume.
3. Try to maximise volume
## Mistakes
The length also affects the volume
## Time Complexity
O(N)
## Space Complexity
O(1)
## Edge Cases
Check between left and right since out of bounds gives you 0 volume!
